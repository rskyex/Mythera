What is Mythera?

A studio that builds explorable interfaces for the structures people live inside but cannot see.

Every culture, institution, algorithm, and literary tradition carries hidden architectures — logics that shape subjectivity, distribute attention, define authenticity, or decide what counts as meaningful. These structures are real, but they operate beneath the threshold of ordinary perception.

Mythera makes those structures explorable. Each project translates an invisible system into an interface you can navigate, inhabit, and think within.

Core principle

The projects are not about hidden structures. They are the hidden structures, made newly legible.

Mythera is a studio for making hidden structures of meaning explorable.

Working across philosophy, literature, selfhood, systems, and cultural interpretation, Mythera builds interfaces where ideas become navigable — where exploration is itself a form of thought.

Each work begins with a question that resists summary. The interfaces make those questions inhabitable.

Works

Strategy Game Realm I

Second Self

A philosophy strategy game about AI, memory, governance, and the kinds of subjects institutions produce. Players navigate institutional systems that shape identity — and discover how little of the self remains untouched.

Interactive Simulation Realm I

Narrative Drift

A simulation of how ordinary algorithmic choices gradually reshape the self. Small decisions accumulate until the person you were becomes unrecognizable — not through crisis, but through drift.

Atlas / Interpretation Interface Realm II

Mishima Atlas

An interactive literary-philosophical atlas mapping Mishima's world across aesthetics, death, performance, politics, and remainder. A navigable architecture of a life that refused to separate art from action.

Literary Interface Realm III

LitPulse

An interface for making the emotional and formal movement of literature visible. Rhythm, tension, pacing, and tone rendered as navigable structure — legible without specialist training.

Music Interface Realm III

Scoreless

A music interface for experiencing structure, tension, and movement without reading a score. The architecture of sound made spatial and intuitive.

Philosophical Simulation Realm I · II

Existential Wager

A philosophical simulation where commitment to ideals gradually exposes contradiction, theatre, and self-mythologization. The wager is whether sincerity survives its own performance.

What is Mythera?

Mythera is a studio that builds explorable interfaces for the structures people live inside but cannot see.

Every culture, every institution, every algorithm, every literary tradition carries hidden architectures — logics that shape subjectivity, distribute attention, define authenticity, or decide what counts as meaningful. These structures are real, but they are rarely visible. They operate beneath the threshold of ordinary perception.

Mythera makes those structures explorable. Each project translates an invisible system — how governance produces subjects, how narrative reshapes the self, how aesthetic form carries emotional logic — into an interface you can navigate, inhabit, and think within.

The chosen forms — games, simulations, atlases, visual essays — are not decorative. They are epistemic. You cannot summarize what an institution does to identity in a paragraph. But you can build a world where someone discovers it by moving through it.

Core principle

The projects are not about hidden structures. They are the hidden structures, made newly legible.
